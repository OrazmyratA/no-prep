No-Prep Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

No-Prep is a classroom material and game app for teachers and learners, for Windows, Android and the web. This page explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and why.

In short

  • No-Prep has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • Your topics, books, pictures and recordings are kept on your own device.
  • Something leaves your device only when you use a feature that needs the internet (listed below), and only for that feature.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

Topics, books, text, images, audio, game results and settings are stored locally on the device (in the app's storage or, on the web, in your browser). On the web, they stay in that one browser: clearing its site data, or using another browser or computer, means they are not there. Exported files are created only when you ask, and you decide where to save or share them.

Features that use the internet

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Link Google Drive, you sign in with Google in your own browser and allow No-Prep to "see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" and to see your email address.

  • No-Prep creates a folder called "No-Prep backup" in your Google Drive and, each time you save a topic, puts a copy of all your topics there (one file, the same as "Export all").
  • No-Prep cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address is shown in the app so you know which account is linked.
  • The files go straight from your device to your own Google Drive. No-Prep has no server copy, and does not share, sell or use this data for anything else.
  • The sign-in is kept only on your device (encrypted with your system's keychain where available). Use Unlink in Quick Links, or myaccount.google.com/permissions, to stop it at any time. Copies already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

No-Prep's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI features (optional)

When you use an AI feature (for example, creating a topic from a book page, preparing tasks, checking writing, speaking practice, pictures or voices), the text, pictures or recordings for that request are sent to the AI service you choose:

  • Your own AI key (Google Gemini, OpenAI or Groq): the request goes directly from your device to that company, under its own privacy terms. Your key is stored only on your device.
  • NoPrep AI (included with a licence): the request and your licence are sent to the NoPrep AI service, which passes the request to an AI company to get the answer. It is used only to answer that request.

Speaking practice can send students' voice recordings for transcription. Please do not include personal information about students in AI requests.

Voices (text to speech)

When the app reads text aloud with an online voice, that text is sent to the voice service (Microsoft Edge voices, or the AI service you chose). Windows' own voices work without the internet.

Video subtitles

Subtitles are made on your device. The first time you use them, the app downloads an open speech model (about 77 MB) from Hugging Face. The video and its sound never leave your device.

Picture search

When you search for pictures, your search words are sent to Pixabay.

Updates and news

The app checks GitHub for new versions and release notes. This sends no personal data beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address).

Licence

A licence is tied to one computer by a code made on that computer from its hardware ID. The check happens on the device; the app does not send this code anywhere. You only share the code if you send it to us to get a licence.

Camera and microphone

No-Prep asks for the camera when you choose to take a photo, and for the microphone when you choose to record. They are used only for that action.

Children

No-Prep is a teacher's tool for the classroom. It has no accounts, chat, social features or ads, and does not collect personal data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer at the support email listed on Google Play.
